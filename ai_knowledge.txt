Artificial Intelligence (AI) is computer science building systems that perform tasks needing human intelligence. Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI where models learn patterns from data. Deep Learning uses multi-layer neural networks. Generative AI creates new content. Large Language Models (LLMs) are neural networks trained on text data. RAG combines information retrieval with an LLM. RAG pipeline steps include document loading, cleaning, chunking, embedding creation, vector DB storage, retrieval, then response generation. Chunking means splitting large documents into smaller parts so they can be embedded and retrieved efficiently.
